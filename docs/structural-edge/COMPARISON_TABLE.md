# Comparison Table — 5 Categories × 5 Criteria

## Scoring methodology

Each category scored 1-5 on five criteria. A score of 5 means most
favourable for a 2-5 person quant research team with $5-50M. Total 5
(uniformly unfavourable) to 25 (uniformly favourable). Justifications
fill each cell; ranking follows scoring.

## Criterion definitions

- Edge: moat strength. 5 = structural and durable (regulatory, physical,
  contractual); 3 = moderately structural with erosion risk; 1 = fragile.
- Capacity: how much a $5-50M book can generate. 5 = scales to $10M+
  annual net; 1 = needs $100M+ to work.
- Capital: inverse — 5 = low capital required; 1 = high. Basis: setup +
  working + regulatory minimum.
- Competencies: fit with research team. 5 = research dominant; 1 =
  ops/trading/compliance dominant (wrong team).
- Time-to-$: 5 = first revenue within 6 months; 1 = 3+ years.

## Per-cell justifications

### Category 1 — Jurisdictional arbitrage

Edge=5: License cost ($400k-2M) plus 6-18 months regulator time plus
ongoing compliance headcount. Strict legal barrier. No algorithmic
substitution.
Capacity=3: $2-5M dealer possible; $10-30M custodian meaningful; $50M
exchange exceeds our upper bound.
Capital=2: $400k-800k setup via Cyprus/Malta + $300-500k per year
compliance + $5-10M regulatory capital for full CASP.
Competencies=2: 60% legal/compliance, 40% ops/infra. Research team
contributes ~15% of workload.
Time=2: 10-14 months to first revenue; 30-36 months to meaningful.

### Category 2 — Physical commodity

Edge=5: Bank lines ($500M-$2B), multi-decade offtake agreements, physical
infrastructure. Highest barrier among all five categories.
Capacity=2: $5-50M covers metals concentrate ($20-30M) or agri niche
($5-15M). Serious physical (oil/LNG) requires $50M+ plus 10-20 ops staff.
Capital=1: $20-30M working capital minimum; $50M+ for meaningful scale.
Competencies=2: 70% physical trading, 20% ops, 10% research.
Time=1: 18-36 months to first trade; 3-5 years to profitability.

### Category 3 — EM local

Edge=4: Relationship plus regulatory, but weaker than physical
infrastructure. Erodes if local competition intensifies.
Capacity=4: $2-5M stake in $200M-$1B AUM local manager. Meaningful
proportional slice of a mid-market player.
Capital=4: $2-5M setup plus $0.5-1M per year local presence.
Competencies=3: 60% local partner sophistication, 40% our research. Team
shifts toward relationship management, not eliminated.
Time=3: 6-12 months operational; 12-24 months to revenue.

### Category 4 — Market infrastructure

Edge=4: Regulatory plus capital moat, but competitive pressure from crypto
venues (10x lower fees) threatens long-term exchange/clearing model.
Capacity=2: $5-15M regulatory capital for direct member; $2-5M for crypto
custodian but different skill set.
Capital=1: $5-15M regulatory (direct); $2-5M crypto custodian plus ops
head count.
Competencies=2: 70% regulatory/ops, 20% tech, 10% research — opposite of
our team profile.
Time=2: 12-24 months setup; 24-36 months to break-even.

### Category 5 — Data exclusivity

Edge=3: Contractual moat, weaker than license or physical infrastructure.
Exclusivity expires when vendor sells to more clients. Enforceability
depends on jurisdiction of vendor.
Capacity=5: Perfect scaling — $1-2M/year feed yields 2-4% alpha on
$20-30M book; add feeds, compounding returns.
Capital=4: $500k-2M/year licensing plus integration; low fixed capital
required.
Competencies=5: 70% research and data integration, 30% contract
negotiation — direct skill match for our team.
Time=4: 3-6 months to first signal; 6-12 months to meaningful alpha.

## Ranking

1. Data exclusivity (21/25) — highest overall. Constrained by weakest
   edge score (3/5), offset by dominating capacity (5/5) and time (4/5).
2. EM local (18/25) — most balanced category. Requires team to shift
   toward relationship management (moderate shift, correct skill set
   required).
3. Jurisdictional arbitrage (14/25) — strong edge (5/5) but low
   competencies (2/5) and slow time (2/5).
4. Physical commodity (11/25) and Market infrastructure (11/25) — tied
   last. Both strong edges (5/5 and 4/5) but the worst capacity, capital,
   and competencies for a 2-5 team with $5-50M.

- Time weighted 2x: Data 25, EM 21, Juris 14, Physical 9, Infra 9

Data exclusivity is robust #1 under every reasonable weighting. No
aggressive reweighting flips the ranking. Recommendation follows in
