# Recommendation

## Context

$5-50M capital, 2-5 person team, research-heavy skill set. Ranking from
COMPARISON_TABLE.md puts Data exclusivity first (21/25), EM local second
(18/25). Recommendation: Data exclusivity primary, EM local as
contingency and parallel track.

## Recommendation #1 — Data exclusivity

### Why

The only category of five where the primary skill required (quantitative
research on novel data) matches our team exactly. Every other category
forces us to hire or partner for 70-80% of the workload. Data exclusivity
uses 70% of what we already have.

Capital efficiency is unmatched. A $1-2M per year licensing budget can
generate 2-4% annualized alpha on a $20-30M book — 20-60% return on
invested capital. Compare: $2-5M setup for a jurisdictional license
produces $500k-1M fee income at scale (20-50% ROI) but takes 30+ months
and requires a different team.

Composability. One working feed teaches the team a repeatable playbook.
Second, third, fourth feed apply the same integration template. After 24
months the team has 3-4 active feeds, compounding edge, and stands as a
de-facto data provider in its niches.

### Four-week plan

**Week 1 — Legal feasibility.** Retain one IP/contract specialist plus one
generalist commercial lawyer in EU and US. Ask for:

- Opinion on 3-year exclusive license term (antitrust risk, data resale
  implications)
- Standard LOI/term sheet template with exclusivity clauses
- GDPR/privacy analysis for the target feed

Cost: $8-15k. Output: 8-12 page memo and approved term sheet framework.

**Week 2 — Partner search.** Screen 15-20 alt-data vendors across three
sectors (shipping/AIS, consumer transaction, on-chain). Tier to 2-3 for
NDA and term sheet. Target criteria:

- Vendor 1-5 years old
- Fewer than 20 existing clients
- Legal basis documented (no resold third-party data)
- Asking price $500k-2M per year

Output: LOI/term sheet with 1-2 vendors with indicative pricing.

**Week 3 — Capital requirement and unit economics.** Model:

- License fee schedule (year 1 plus option years)
- Integration cost (data engineers, storage, backtest infra) — $300-500k
  one-time
- Team additions (2 data engineers or in-house) — $200-300k per year
- Revenue: expected alpha (2-4% on $20-30M) plus resale ($100-300k/year)

Output: 3-scenario memo (base/bull/bear). Gating metric: realized Sharpe
above 1.0 on paper trading after integration.

**Week 4 — Go/no-go decision.** Decision matrix:

- GO if: two vendors ready for LOI, projected ROI >3x, legal memo clears
  showstoppers, team commits to 24-month build
- NO-GO if: only one viable vendor, projected ROI <2x, legal memo flags
  material risk, team uncertain about commitment

Output: signed decision memo. If GO — Week 5 begins LOI negotiation and
paid trial. If NO-GO — pivot to Recommendation #2 within two weeks.

### Kill criteria (Recommendation #1)

1. Feed commoditization. If 2+ additional vendors offer the same
   underlying data within 6 months of our first use, edge collapses. We
   need 1-2 vendor exclusivity holding for 6+ months post-signature.

2. Alpha non-emergence. If realized Sharpe on paper-traded model trained
   on the data <1.0 after 6 months of live integration, economics fail.
   Data cleaning takes time, but 6 months is realistic.

3. Legal revocation. If feed legal basis is challenged (GDPR complaint,
   ITC takedown, regulator action) within 12 months of licensing, the
   deal becomes worthless. Cap exposure at one license fee.

## Recommendation #2 — EM local (contingency)

### Why

Second-highest ranked (18/25), complementary in nature: relationship-heavy
rather than data-heavy. If Data exclusivity fails legal or economic
feasibility in Week 4, EM local is executable in 6-12 months with existing
team capital.

Position as sub-advisory to a small local manager (30-49% stake) in
Vietnam (VinaCapital/Dragon scale, but smaller) or Brazil (CVM-licensed
regional). Team brings research, tactical allocation, foreign capital
access; partner brings local ops, compliance, distribution.

Capital: $2-5M stake plus $0.5-1M per year local presence — below Data
exclusivity entry cost but with lower alpha ceiling.

## Categories not to pursue in current shape

- Physical commodity trading: $20-50M entry for metals, requires 2-3
  senior hires from majors, 18-36 months to first trade. Fails
  capacity-for-2-5-team criterion.
- Market infrastructure (exchange/clearing): direct membership requires
  $5-15M regulatory capital, 12-24 months, different skill set. Crypto
  custody a partial exception ($2-5M) but a business-model shift, not a
  research strategy.
- Jurisdictional arbitrage: strong edge, but 60-70% of work is
  compliance/legal — not our team's core. Best pursued as co-investment
  with a licensed operator, not as primary.

## Next steps (within 30 days)

- Days 1-3: executive decision on primary pursuit — Data exclusivity vs.
  EM local vs. parallel
- Days 4-7: engage external legal (Data) or partner scan (EM) — parallel
  tracks are safe
- Days 8-14: first vendor NDAs / first partner introductions
- Days 15-21: first LOI / first term sheet
- Days 22-30: go/no-go per Week-4 criteria

If primary track stalls before Week 4 decision, switch to Recommendation
#2 without re-analysis. Both are validated against the ranking. What
matters is the first three months of execution — the winner is determined
by who signs the first LOI or NDA, not by which category ranks highest on
paper.
